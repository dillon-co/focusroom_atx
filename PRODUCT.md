# The Focus Room — product

**What it is.** A private, coach-led training room at 2709 East 5th Street, Suite 110A, East Austin (78702). It trains three things together: Muscle, Mindset, Mission. Founded by Jarred Curcio (head coach) and Kate Curcio (co-founder, systems and culture). Headquarters of True Fitness and Focus.

**Who it is for.** People who have started programs before and want to sustain: The Builder, The Operator, The Seeker, The Return, The Voice, The Unseen.

**The one action.** Apply. There is no checkout and no free trial: you apply, have a conversation, and qualified applicants start with the cohort on the first of the month.

**Constraints that define it.** Membership capped at thirty. Small groups capped at four. By conversation only.

**Proof that exists.** Jarred: 20 years, zero missed workouts; natural bodybuilding champion at 21 after weighing 300 lb at 19; Penn State Law JD; Austin Under 40 finalist 2026. Kate: registered nurse, 20+ years in healthcare leadership. The real room (navy walls, gold racks, arched mirrors).

**Voice.** Direct, disciplined, warm underneath. Short declaratives. Stoic references. Never hype words.

**Brand.** Ink #0e0c08, bone #ede7da, gold #c8952e (gold-lite #e3b85c), navy #0a1424, steel #8496ac. Archivo.

**Mode.** Persuade.
